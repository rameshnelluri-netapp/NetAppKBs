Introduction

One of the most common questions from NetApp administrators moving to AWS is:

"My FSx file system shows 3072 Provisioned IOPS. Does that mean my workload is limited to 3072 IOPS?"

The answer is usually No. Provisioned IOPS represents backend SSD performance allocation and should not be confused with client-visible NFS, SMB, or iSCSI operations.

AWS abstracts the hardware layer. You purchase performance through service-level attributes rather than controller hardware.

What Does 3072 Provisioned IOPS Mean?

Suppose AWS displays: 

SSD Capacity      : 10 TB
Provisioned IOPS  : 3072
Throughput        : 512 MB/s

This means AWS guarantees backend SSD performance allocation equivalent to approximately 3072 SSD IOPS.

It does NOT automatically mean clients can perform only 3072 NFS or SMB operations.
